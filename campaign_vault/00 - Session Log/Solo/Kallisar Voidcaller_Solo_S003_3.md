## Solo Adventure Log

### Turn 0
**Player:** [Session Start]
**DM:** The bitter taste of Victor's words still lingers, a foulness that even the thick, salty air of the Dock Ward can't wash away. You sought solitude in the city's grimiest, most chaotic corner, hoping the press of sailors, gamblers, and thieves would be anonymous enough to swallow your anger. Now, you find yourself tucked away in a narrow passage between two leaning warehouses, the reek of brine and bilge water thick in your throat. A single oil lantern swings from a rusty hook overhead, its light flickering across cobblestones slick with fish guts and something worse. The distant sound of a tavern brawl is a dull roar, punctuated by the closer, more intimate sounds of dripping water and the scuttling of unseen things.

From the mouth of the alley, you overhear a hushed, tense exchange. Two figures, little more than silhouettes against the dim light of the main thoroughfare, are speaking in clipped whispers. "...the *Sea Serpent* docks at moonrise," one voice hisses, rough as gravel. "The package has to be on it. No excuses. The Zhentarim don't tolerate mistakes." The other figure just nods, a quick, jerky motion, before they both melt away in opposite directions, swallowed by the night.

The sudden silence they leave behind is broken by a new sound. A fat, grey rat scurries from beneath a pile of discarded nets, dragging something that catches the lantern's glow. It struggles with the object for a moment before dropping it with a soft *clink* on the stones not ten feet from you. The object shines with the unmistakable lustre of silver. The rat gives a frustrated squeak and vanishes into a crack in the foundation, leaving the small treasure gleaming in the filth.

The silver trinket lies waiting, an easy prize. The whispers of a clandestine deal and the name of a ship hang in the air, a thread of intrigue waiting to be pulled. The alley is still, the shadows deep. What do you do?
