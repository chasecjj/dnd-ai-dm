## Solo Adventure Log

### Turn 0
**Player:** [Session Start]
**DM:** Hadrian separated from the party at the Waterdeep Docks to scout for Fingers Finnigan. From a perch atop damp crates, he spotted two figures in a shadowed alley — one hulking, one small and frightened.

### Turn 1
**Player:** I sneak closer to the alley to eavesdrop
**DM:** Hadrian crept into position and overheard the conversation. The smaller figure is Finn — the orphan who taught Hadrian to survive on the streets. The hulking enforcer, Grigor, is collecting a debt for the Xanathar Guild. Grigor sensed something and called out into the shadows.

### Turn 2
**Player:** I emerge from the shadows and bluff, claiming to work for "the boss," pointing at a dropped note as proof I have inside knowledge.
**DM:** Deception 16. Grigor was skeptical but unsettled. He demanded to know who Hadrian was, giving him five seconds. Finn recognized Hadrian but stayed silent, terrified.

### Turn 3
**Player:** I stand my ground and suggest the boss doesn't tell Grigor everything — implying I'm on the inside of a circle Grigor isn't.
**DM:** Deception 21. The bluff landed hard. Grigor's certainty cracked — paranoid doubt flickered across his face. Then the gas lamps in the alley sputtered and died, plunging everything into darkness. Grigor snarled into the dark, unnerved. Hadrian has Darkvision. Grigor doesn't.

### Turn 4
**Player:** I say something sinister about the boss having more to share if Grigor survives "this test." I activate Darkvision and circle around to plan my attack.
**DM:** Deception 20. Hadrian framed the darkness as a deliberate test from the Xanathar Guild boss. Grigor lowered his weapon, stammering about not being warned. Hadrian circled through the dark alley, using Darkvision to scout positions while Grigor stumbled blind. A wet, shuffling sound echoed from deeper in the alley.
