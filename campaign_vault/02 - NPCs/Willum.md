# Willum
## Description
A slender, beardless youth. Nervous demeanor, wide-eyed, easily overwhelmed. Was nursing a watery ale at the Yawning Portal.

## Personality
Timid and easily flustered. Stammers when pressured. Seems out of his depth among hardened adventurers.

## Background
Purchased a mysterious map from someone down by the docks. The map is covered in strange symbols and faded ink, and appears to be quite old.

## Secret
_Unknown — the origin and true nature of his map may be significant._

## Connections
- Bought his map from an unknown seller at the docks.
- His map is similar to but older than Bruenor's map.

## Party Relationship
Overwhelmed by the party's aggressive recruitment attempts. Sigfried nearly won him over with charm. Victor's aggressive behavior put him off. Status as a companion is uncertain.

## Plot Hooks
- His map could be a key to finding the smuggler tunnels or a deeper entrance to Undermountain.
- The person who sold him the map at the docks could be a lead.

## DM Notes
- May or may not be following the party to the docks. His willingness is questionable after Victor's intimidation.
- If he doesn't come willingly, the party still knows about the map's existence and its dock origin.
