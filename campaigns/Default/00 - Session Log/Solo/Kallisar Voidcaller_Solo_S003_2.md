You sought solitude here, in the chaotic heart of the city's underbelly, your keen eyes constantly scanning for opportunity, for distraction, for anything of value.

The docks are a symphony of noise and scent. Salt spray mists your face, mixing with the stink of drying fish, hot tar, and the distant, sweet perfume of spices from some faraway land. Gulls shriek overhead, their cries lost in the creak of strained ropes, the rhythmic crash of waves against barnacled pilings, and the shouts of sailors and porters. You move with a practiced ease, a shadow in the twilight, your gaze sweeping over the scene. Near a stack of crates, your eyes catch a furtive exchange: a shifty-eyed man in a patched coat passes a small, heavy-looking package to a burly sailor. The transaction is over in a blink, the two figures melting back into the crowd.

A moment later, a sharp, distressed cry cuts through the din, coming from a narrow, refuse-choked alley between two towering warehouses. It sounds like a young woman, her voice abruptly muffled, followed by the sound of a brief scuffle. As the echo of that cry fades, your foot nearly brushes against something half-buried in the grime near a coil of old rope. A glint of tarnished silver. It’s a locket, its chain snapped, bearing a faint, unfamiliar symbol on its surface.

The muffled struggle from the alley has gone quiet. The two figures from the suspicious deal are lost to the throng. The locket lies waiting in the muck at your feet. The labyrinth of the docks presents its choices. What do you do?
